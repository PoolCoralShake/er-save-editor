How it works

From download to edited save in three steps

No installers, no dependencies — the editor is a single portable executable.

1

Download and launch

Grab the latest release from gitscore.cc, extract the archive, and run er-save-editor.exe. Windows 10 and 11 are supported out of the box.

2

Open your character save

Close ELDEN RING, then point the editor at your save file. It reads your characters and automatically creates a backup before any change is written.

3

Edit and save

Adjust stats, runes, items, weapons and armor, hit Save, and relaunch the game. Your updated Tarnished loads exactly as configured.

FAQ

Questions from fellow Tarnished

Short answers about safety, compatibility and getting started.

Is er-save-editor free to use?+
Yes. er-save-editor is completely free and open source. There is no premium tier, no account and no telemetry — download it from gitscore.cc and use it as much as you like.
Will editing my save get me banned online?+
Edited saves should be treated as offline-only. Use them in single-player or on a separate profile, and do not bring modified characters into online play. Always keep the automatic backup the editor creates so you can restore your original save.
